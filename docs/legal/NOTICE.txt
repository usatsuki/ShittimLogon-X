  Modifications: fixed axes opsz=72, wdth=100, wght=900, ROND=20, slnt=0;

stb_vorbis
----------

  Original factory voice recordings are preserved as Ogg Vorbis, without a
  further lossy conversion. A voice-only decoder avoids dependence on optional
  Windows Store codecs. It accepts only the exact reviewed factory bytes;
  legacy MP3 voice packs and all BGM retain their existing decoding paths.
  This format change does not grant rights in the game recordings.

  Development location: vendor/stb/stb_vorbis.c (unmodified upstream).

Offline city data and solar scheduling
-------------------------------------

  City coordinates and identifiers are derived from GeoNames:
  https://www.geonames.org/ and https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The selected city subset adds edited four-language display names, search
  aliases, stable application IDs and Windows time-zone identifiers. GeoNames
  does not endorse this application. The data remains under its own license;
  Shittim Logon X's proprietary program restrictions do not override those rights.
  Full license and attribution: GEONAMES-LICENSE.txt.
  Source data provenance and selection details are retained with the source.

  Windows time-zone mappings are derived from the Unicode Common Locale Data
  Repository (CLDR), Copyright Unicode, Inc., under the Unicode License v3.
  https://cldr.unicode.org/ and https://www.unicode.org/license.txt
  The mapping is a selected and adapted subset. Its license and attribution
  are also preserved in GEONAMES-LICENSE.txt.

  Sunrise and sunset are calculated locally from the selected city's coordinates
  and date. Astronomical method reference:
  https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html
  The implementation does not call GeoNames, NOAA, weather or location services.
  Calculated times are approximate; terrain and atmospheric conditions can
  change observed sunrise/sunset. No affiliation or certification is implied.
